- ``derive_graph`` derives a document's outline from the ``heading_path``
  that ``StructureChunker`` records on every chunk: one ``heading`` node
  (``NodeKind.HEADING``) per source and heading path, ``contains`` and
  ``parent_of`` edges between a heading, its sub-headings and the documents
  directly under it, and ``same_source`` from each top heading to its
  source. A traversal can therefore walk from a chunk to its section, its
  sibling chunks and the rest of the outline. Nothing is stored, and
  documents without a heading path give the same graph as before.
