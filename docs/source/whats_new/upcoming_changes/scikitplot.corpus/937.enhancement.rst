- Every corpus reader takes ``max_text_chars`` and ``on_text_limit``
  (``"refuse"`` or ``"truncate"``): a bound on the text one input yields,
  counted as chunks stream at the one place every chunk passes, custom
  extractors and archive members included. It is off by default, except
  for the formats where a small file can expand: ``OfficeReader`` (a cell
  repeated across columns and rows: 483 bytes could yield 34 billion
  characters) and ``PDFReader`` (pages sharing one compressed stream:
  4 KB yielded 4 million characters) default to 256 Mi characters.
  ``ZipReader`` bounds each member by its own reader and the archive total
  by its own setting; a truncated chunk is marked ``text_truncated``.
