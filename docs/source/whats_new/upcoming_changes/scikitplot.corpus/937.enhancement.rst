- Every corpus reader takes ``max_text_chars``, ``max_text_chars_per_byte``
  and ``on_text_limit`` (``"refuse"`` or ``"truncate"``): a bound on the
  text one input yields, counted as chunks stream at the one place every
  chunk passes, custom extractors and archive members included. The limit
  is the smaller of ``max_text_chars`` and
  ``max(max_text_chars_per_byte x file size, 1 Mi)`` characters, so output,
  and the work of extracting it, stay in proportion to the input. Both are
  off by default, except where a small file can expand: ``OfficeReader``
  (a cell repeated across columns and rows: 483 bytes could yield 34
  billion characters) and ``PDFReader`` (pages sharing one compressed
  stream: 4 KB yielded 4 million characters) default to 256 Mi characters
  and 50 characters per byte. ``ZipReader`` bounds each member by its own
  reader and the archive total by its own setting; a truncated chunk is
  marked ``text_truncated``.
