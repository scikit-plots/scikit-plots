- Markup patterns no longer cost quadratic time on text full of
  unclosed ``<`` or ``{``: ``HTMLStripNormalizer``, the YouTube
  transcript cleaner, the CHM contents parser and the subtitle cleaners
  each stop a tag at the next opener (``<[^<>]+>``), and CHM ``<object>``
  blocks are paired in one pass. 400 kB of ``<`` now takes milliseconds
  (it was over a minute), and a lone ``<`` keeps the text after it:
  ``x < y and <b>bold</b>`` becomes ``x < y and bold``, where
  ``< y and`` was deleted. Also lands the subtitle review fixes merged
  without them in #983: companion lookup in 0.14 s instead of about
  330 s for 5000 videos, ``companion_options`` on ``VideoReader`` and
  ``AudioReader``, and the encoding policy for plain-text companions. A
  static check (``sweep_static.py --fail-on regex-unbounded-scan``, now
  in CI) refuses the pattern shape anywhere in first-party code.
